● Based on the codebase, here's how the different speech types are analyzed in Speakflow:

  Core Analysis Framework

  All speeches (general, interview, presentation, sales) are analyzed using the same 22-metric system, evaluating both
  technical vocal qualities and content delivery:

  The 22 Core Metrics

  1. Transcription — Word-for-word accuracy
  2. Pronunciation — Mispronounced words with correction suggestions
  3. Pace (WPM) — Ideal: 115–165 words/min (too slow <115, too fast >165)
  4. Filler Words — Counts of "um," "uh," "like," etc.
  5. Confidence Score — Overall speaker assurance (0–100%)
  6. Rhythm — Speech flow and timing consistency (0–100%)
  7. Intonation — Pitch variation and expressiveness (0–100%)
  8. Articulation — Clarity of consonants/vowels (0–100%)
  9. Breath Management — Breathing technique quality (0–100%)
  10. Vocal Health — Strain level and vocal fry detection
  11. Emotional Tone — Primary emotion + intensity
  12. Sentiment Score — Overall positivity/negativity (0–100%)
  13. Vocal Resonance — Sound quality and projection (0–100%)
  14. Micro-Hesitations — Sub-500ms pauses (count)
  15. Plosive Analysis — /p/, /b/, /t/ clarity
  16. Environmental Noise — Background noise level (0–100%)
  17. Accent Profile — Detected accent + clarity score (0–100%)
  18. Stress Profile — Stress level, nervousness indicators, peak moments
  19. Clinical Insights — Speech pathologist perspective
  20. General Advice — 3–5 actionable coaching tips
  21. Phrasingfeedback — How well you group ideas together
  22. Plosive Analysis — Emphasis on clarity of hard consonants

  Mode-Specific Professional Benchmarks

  A professional would adjust slightly by mode:

  Interview Mode (Professional at 90/100)

  - Confidence: 90–94% (composed under pressure, not overconfident)
  - Stress: 10–20 (controlled nervous energy → credibility)
  - Pace: 125–140 WPM (slightly slower for thoughtfulness)
  - Filler Words: 0–1 (structured, "give me a second" beats "um")

  Presentation Mode (Professional at 92/100)

  - Intonation: 90–96% (more dynamic; emphasis on key points)
  - Rhythm: 88–95% (varied pacing to highlight sections)
  - Articulation: 94–98% (technical terms pronounced perfectly)
  - Pace: 130–150 WPM (varies with importance of content)

  Sales Pitch Mode (Professional at 91/100)

  - Confidence: 92–96% (persuasive authority)
  - Emotional Tone: "Enthusiastic/Positive" at 85–95% intensity
  - Sentiment: 75–85% (energized, not manic)
  - Pace: 140–160 WPM (energetic, momentum-building)
  - Filler Words: 0–1 (no hesitation; conviction)

  General Mode (Professional at 90/100)

  - All metrics: Balanced 85–95% across the board
  - Focus: consistency, clarity, professionalism

  ---
  What Would Pull a Professional's Score Down?

  Even pros aren't perfect. Common reasons for sub-benchmark scores:

  ❌ External factors:
  - Poor audio quality → lower Environmental Noise clarity
  - Unfamiliar topic → slightly higher Micro-Hesitations
  - Distracting environment → elevated Stress profile

  ❌ Technical slips:
  - One mispronounced word → Pronunciation feedback, but still overall 95%+ confidence
  - Rushed delivery → Articulation drops to 85–90%
  - Monotone section → Intonation dips to 75–85%
